How matching works

Published rules, not hidden predictions.

Every path has its own relevant assessment-category weights. Its objective fit is 80% of the final score. Alignment with the three self-reported interest answers is the remaining 20%. Interest alignment is a preference signal only, never evidence of skill. Scores are rounded to whole percentages and ranked by score, then name for ties.
